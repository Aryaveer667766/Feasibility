Questions

Before you join

Do I need to give up rice, roti or sweets?

No. Plans work with the food you already eat. Your coach adjusts portions and timing so you can keep your favourites in moderation.

How soon will I see results?

Most clients notice better energy and digestion in two weeks. Healthy fat loss is usually 0.5 to 1 kg per week.

Can I follow a plan if I am vegetarian or Jain?

Yes. Every diet chart is built around your food preferences, allergies and religious or cultural rules.

Are your supplements safe to take with medicines?

Check with your doctor first if you take regular medication. Our nutritionists can review ingredients with you.

Do you coach online?

Yes. Calls, diet charts and chat support all work online, so you can join from any city.
